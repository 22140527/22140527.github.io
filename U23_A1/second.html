Computer Systems

Planning

Planning for a computer is very important becuase it helps you avoid unwanted problems can minimize damage save time and will save money.

Possible risks

Installing the components in the wrong way can damage them severly or even completely break them you must do research and know the right tools, procedures and safety.

Risks like overheating or not enough power from the power supply can lead to bad fan usage causing overheating and can lead to crashes or slow loading speed and even long term damage for the computer since there is not enough power and bad airflow to keep the components cool by planning before it can stop the risks from happening ensuring your system runs safely and effeciently so you get your moneys worth.

Price / Budget

A budget is always important thing to have in mind becuase it decides what components you buy and it helps you stay in control of spending it helps you make smart decisions on not wasting all of your money and avoids problems during the project.

Without a budget it is easy to get carried away and buy the best of best parts that you may not even need this is why it is crucial to have a budget in mind to be resposible of spending and ensuring you have enough for every part

Apart from the components you also need to buy a operating system ( Windows license ) you need thermal paste for the CPU need to spend money for shipping a budget gives you enough money for these expenses

Knowing a budget makes it easier to compare between values and between different parts ( brands and models ) that fits within your price range
